## Rules for the shoot

1. This is a real demonstration of the system and its recorded evidence, not a slide narrative. Every shot shows the system, its files, its logs or a replay running. Title cards and diagrams carry no claim that an on-screen file does not carry.
2. Open with the one-line pre-existing/new statement (shot 1). Organizer rule: the video must open with one line saying what pre-existed and what was built. Failure to identify original contributions is grounds for disqualification.
3. Every shot is tied to evidence that exists today, or is marked `PENDING: needs real run`. A `PENDING` shot is filmed only after the real run exists. Never act out a run, never narrate a hypothetical log as if it were recorded, never show a mock terminal as evidence.
4. The hand-run comparison is a separate, labelled section and says HAND-RUN on screen and in voiceover. The two-loop result is shown as the final aggregate E1 table plus ledger entries: original fresh 55.0% / 76.4%, candidate screening 52.0% / 75.4% (say it selected the candidate) and fresh 47.0% / 70.1%, stock 63.0% / 75.4% labelled "context only, not SFT" on screen. Say out loud, in this order: quality (scores) did not improve on this evidence, reliability (completed adapters) did, one trial against two, exploratory, no improvement claim. Failed trials (spread ledger zeroes with both reasons, `t-0005`, H1 `t-0008`–`t-0010`) are called failures, never 0% scores; rows that never ran read "not run". No favourable spin: do not lead with stock, do not average the two candidate trials into a win, do not call the screen a fresh trial.
5. Do not describe the Muse API or Baseten routes as tested. If they are mentioned at all, the words are "planned, not tested".
6. Show no secrets, tokens, private examples or hidden evaluation data. Selection and test data, and raw private scorer logs, never appear on screen. Screen-share only sanitized, public-safe files (see `EVIDENCE_MANIFEST.md`).
7. Voiceover in English, plain words, numbers quoted exactly. Where the script gives a number, it comes from the verified facts; do not round it on the day.

Timing note: 180 seconds total, one continuous plan (no alternate endings). Shots 1–8 fill 0:00 to about 2:20; the final 40 seconds (2:20 to 3:00) carry the candidate diff, the final E1 table read honestly, the incomplete confirmation, the failed H1 attempt with both causes, the not-run test row and the final spend. Shot 1 (the opening pre-existing/new line) does not move. The final numbers below are fixed from the 2026-11-04 13:48 ET update, not read off a still-running search; still confirm each artefact against the sanitized export on recording day. If the sanitized export of any artefact below does not exist on recording day, show its `REPORT.md`/`EVIDENCE_MANIFEST.md` row instead and say the export is pending — never stage a log.
